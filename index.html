Main janta hu sabki apni life aur space hoti hai. Mera irada kabhi aapko pareshan karna nahi hota, bas ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai aapse baat karke, isliye khud ko rok nahi pata.

Thodi Si Guidance...

Main aapse apni problems share kiya karunga, par please mujhe sahi guide karna ki kya karna chahiye aur kya nahi. Abhi meri life mein bahut mess hi chal raha hai... family mein bhi kaafi cheezein uljhi hui hain.

Mujhe hi sab kuch handle karna pad raha hai, lekin sach batau toh mujhe kuch samajh nahi aa raha ki main kya karu aur kya nahi. Main har waqt bas confused sa rehta hu.

Mujhe umeed hai ki aap thoda guide kar doge... kyunki kehte hain na ki kabhi kabhi hum khud akele cheezon ko soch-samajh nahi paate. Lekin agar kisi apne se share karein, toh wo hamari situation samajh kar hume sahi raasta dikha sakta hai.
