Flashback: College Ke Din ⏪

Helo madam ji... Toh baat hai un dino ki jab college mein maine admission liya. Thoda sa flashback mein aata hu. Mujhe na 'love at first sight' hua tha school time mein hi... 😍 aur main us ladki ko dhundta tha, hamesha mere mind se nahi nikalti thi wo.

Fir jab college gaya, tab first year mein meri class mein ek ladki thi Sakshi. Sabse pehli ladki jisse maine baat ki as a friend. Wahan bhi wahi 'Jaat' wala scene tha ki Jaat gusse wale hote hain. 🚩 Main college ke first day bilkul shant tha, kisi se koi baat nahi ki. Chup chap baitha raha, thoda nervous tha.

Tab Sakshi aayi aur boli, "Hello, mera naam Sakshi hai." Maine apna naam bataya aur fir first bench pe hi baith gaya. Disha (Bhabhi) aur Kanika (Bua ki ladki) dono same college mein hi thi, mujhe wahan zabardasti bheja gaya tha mera koi interest nahi tha. 😒

Page 1

Ek Thappad Aur Naye Dost ✋💥

Class mein dheere dheere sab ek dusre ko janne lage. Ek ladka tha Shashank Pandey aur uska dost Mohit Rana. Un dono ne ek din Disha ko kuch ulta seedha keh diya. 😡 Disha ne Kanika ko bataya aur boli mujhe na batayein, par mujhe pata chal gaya.

College shuru hue 1 month bhi nahi hua tha. Chutti ka time tha, Pandey bus mein tha, maine gusse mein usko bola ki "Tu kal miliye." Next day main toh bhul gaya par Pandey ko yaad tha. Wo Mohit ke sath aaya aur bola "Haan re tu ke keh raha tha..."

Maine utha, usko samne khada kiya aur khinch ke ek rapta mara uske muh pe! 💥 Mohit khada dekhta raha. Sab chup chap dekhne lage. Maine bas itna kaha, "Kal jise tune cheda tha wo bhabhi hai meri, jaakar sorry bol de," aur main class se bahar chala gaya. Usne sorry bol diya. 😤

Page 2

Dosti Ya Matlabb? 🤝

Maine class attend karni chhod di thi. 7-8 days baad gaya toh last bench pe baitha. Wahan mujhe Rahul, Rohit, Prakash, Deepak, Ankush naye dost mile. Rahul Yadav Sakshi ko pasand karta tha, par usko laga ki Sakshi mujhe pasand karti hai.

Maine usse share kiya tha ki main kisi aur ko dhund raha hu jiska naam bhi nahi pata. Par college mein strike hui, maine full participate kiya, tod fod ki... aur mera naam uchal gaya "Jaat jaat" karke. 🌪️

Isi baat ka fayda Rahul Yadav ne uthaya. Sakshi ko laga main usko pasand karne laga hu aur usne mujhse baat band kar di. Rahul ne usko bhadkaya ki Jaat toh aisa hi gusse wala hai. Wo clever tha, usne mauke ka fayda utha liya. 🧠
Page 3

Dhokhe Aur Snake 🐍

Final year mein dono relation mein aa gaye aur unhone mujhse dosti khatam kar di. Unki condition thi ki relation mein aana hai toh mujhse baat band karni padegi. Jab Jharsa ke bacho se unka jhagda hua, tab bhi main akela gaya tha unke liye... jabki unhone mujhe batana bhi chhod diya tha. 😔

Second year mein mujhe Avdesh Rana mila (mera junior), kuldeep (Rajput), aur Dhanraj (Gujjar). Hum 4 dost bane, sab sath ghumte the. Mera asool tha ki masti karlo par kisi ladki ko mat chedo. 🛡️

Avdesh ko maine real brother ki tarah treat kiya tha... par life ki sabse badi chot usine mujhe di. Main aaj bhi pachtata hu ki usko bhai kyun banaya. Khair, dost banaye bahut, par jaise hi mauka mila, sabne fayda uthaya. 🥀
Page 4

Pehla Pyaar Aur Bebasi ❤️‍🩹

Finally, mujhe wo school wali ladki dikhi! 🥹 Mere cousin ki jaan pehchan ki thi. FB pe add hua, baat hui, aur maine propose kar diya. Usne haan bhi kar di. Par 6-8 months baad usne mujhe bola ki "Main Aman se breakup karke aa gayi hu." 🤯

Aman, jisko wo apna best friend batati thi, wo uska BF nikla. Main emotional fool tha, pyaar mein andha... uski baaton mein aa gaya. Humari ladaiyan hoti, par main sary bolta. Main usko khone se darta tha. Maine mummy se bhi milwaya tha, shadi karna chahta tha usse. 💍

Main Laxmi Nagar tak gaya hu ladne uske liye. Ladko se attraction paana uski fitrat mein tha, par main samajh nahi paya. Wo mujhe kutte ki tarah aage peeche ghumati, par main pagal tha uske liye. 🐕‍🦺

Page 5

Toota Dil Aur Sabak 💔

Phir Tau ji ne mere peeche ek ladki lagwa di, zabardasti rishta pakka kar diya. Din raat kalesh hota. Main lada sabse, finally us ladki se peecha chuta. Par jab chuta, tab tak meri GF kisi aur ki ho chuki thi... 💔

6 saal ka pyaar tha meri taraf se, aur 2 saal se wo already kisi aur ke sath relation mein thi. Aur tab bhi mujhe lagta meri hi galti hai. Wo din aaya jab mera dil toota... aur bahut bura haal hua mera. 😭

Avdesh jisko bhai bolta tha, uske samne baith kar bahut roya. Par ultimate wo bhi meri life mein ek saap nikla. Usne peeth mein aisa khanjar mara jiska zakham aaj tak nahi bhara.

Gussa itna hai ki fod du, par chot khate khate main aisa ban gaya hu ki... 😶
Baaki details mil kar call pe bataunga... 📞
